<h1 align="center">
  About Me
</h1>

I am Javier Moya Payà (born in 1982 in Alcoy, Spain), and I hold a Ph.D. in Telecommunications Engineering from the Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain), where I also obtained my B.Sc. (2007) and M.Sc. (2008) degrees in Telecommunications Engineering and Signal Processing and Communications, respectively.

I conducted my doctoral research at the Center for Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering (CBET, UPV) and the Spanish National Research Council, focusing on statistical signal processing and neuroimaging.

In 2012, I joined the Lucerne School of Engineering and Architecture (Switzerland), where I worked as a Senior R&D Engineer at the Competence Center for Intelligent Sensor Networks, developing advanced digital signal processing systems and communication protocols on FPGA-based platforms.

In 2018, I joined GRAPIN AG (formerly DC-ONE AG), a startup in high-performance cloud computing. As CTO and Head of Product, I led the development of reconfigurable cloud platforms leveraging FPGA accelerators as cloud-native computing resources.

From 2021 to 2025, I was a Senior Scientist at ETH Zurich, where I designed and deployed the Heterogeneous Accelerated Compute Cluster (ETHZ-HACC) under the umbrella of the AMD University Program. As part of this work, I led the development of infrastructure automation and deployment frameworks for heterogeneous accelerated systems, focusing on reproducible environments for HPC and AI workloads. This included the development of HACC Management and HACC Development, a workflow orchestration platform integrating CPUs, GPUs, FPGAs, and high-performance networking technologies. With these tools, the ETHZ-HACC became the most widely used HACC within the program, supporting several hundred users worldwide for research and development in heterogeneous acceleration and distributed computing.

In 2021, I founded [Oreol KLG](https://oreol.ch) to provide consultancy services to companies with advanced acceleration and high-performance computing requirements. My recent work focuses on sustainable edge-to-core computing platforms for HPC and AI workloads, with particular emphasis on immersion-cooled infrastructures, distributed accelerated systems, and private heterogeneous computing environments. This work includes partnerships with companies such as Submer, 2CRSi, or Tailscale, as well as continued collaboration with ETH Zurich on heterogeneous accelerated computing initiatives.
