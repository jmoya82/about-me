<h1 align="center">
  About Me
</h1>

I am Javier Moya Payà (born in 1982 in Alcoy, Spain), and I hold a Ph.D. in Telecommunications Engineering from the Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain), where I also obtained my B.Sc. (2007) and M.Sc. (2008) degrees in Telecommunications Engineering and Signal Processing and Communications, respectively.

I conducted my doctoral research at the Center for Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering (CBET, UPV) and the Spanish National Research Council, focusing on statistical signal processing and neuroimaging.

In 2012, I joined the Lucerne School of Engineering and Architecture (Switzerland), where I worked as a Senior R&D Engineer at the Competence Center for Intelligent Sensor Networks, developing advanced digital signal processing systems and communication protocols on FPGA-based platforms.

In 2018, I joined GRAPIN AG (formerly DC-ONE AG), a startup in high-performance cloud computing. As CTO and Head of Product, I led the development of reconfigurable cloud platforms leveraging FPGA accelerators as cloud-native computing resources.

From 2021 to 2025, I was a Senior Scientist at the Systems Group of ETH Zurich (ETHZ), where I designed and deployed its Heterogeneous Accelerated Compute Cluster (ETHZ-HACC) within the AMD University Program.

I am currently Director of Hardware Technologies at BE BRAVE AG (Switzerland), where I am responsible for the design and deployment of data-centric AI infrastructure, including immersion-cooled accelerated datacenters and edge-to-core computing platforms, combined with AI orchestration platforms to deliver acceleration-as-a-service solutions in partnership with hosted·ai.
